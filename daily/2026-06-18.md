### Concepts I Can Explain

- Why a local model directory plus `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1` avoids network variance and container-side Hugging Face failures.
- Why TTFT, TPOT, latency, throughput, memory, and failure rate all matter together.
- Why concurrency increases throughput but can raise TTFT and latency.
- Why throughput under concurrency should use wall-clock time, not summed per-request latency.
- Why warmup requests should be outside the measured benchmark window.
- Why token throughput should use server-reported completion tokens.
- Why repeated prompts can activate prefix cache and hide prefill cost.
- Why `max_tokens` is only an upper bound; the model may stop earlier.

### Confusions / Mistakes

- The first concurrent benchmark counted warmup time in measured wall time, which underestimated throughput.
- The first token count used whitespace estimation, which was not rigorous enough for token throughput.
